# Tuition Contribution (Katkı Payı), Tuition Fees and Refunds

- Source: BŞEÜ Student Affairs (Öğrenci İşleri Daire Başkanlığı), Frequently Asked Questions: Harç İşlemleri
- Link: https://bilecik.edu.tr/ogrenciisleri/Icerik/HARc_isLEMLERi
- Responsible office: Student Affairs, Fees Office (Harçlar Şube Müdürlüğü) (0228 214 10 71, ogrenciisleri@bilecik.edu.tr)
- Note: This is an English summary of the Turkish FAQ; the Turkish text is binding. Amounts change every year; check the Student Affairs announcements for current amounts.

## Who pays the tuition contribution
Tuition contributions and fees are set every year by Presidential Decree. First-education (daytime) associate and bachelor students who continue within the normal study period do not pay a contribution. Students who cannot graduate within that period pay a contribution. Second-education (evening) students pay a tuition fee.

## Registering in a second program
The state covers the contribution for only one program a student is registered in. A student who registers in a second higher-education program (except double major programs) pays the contribution for the second program themselves.

## If you do not pay on time
The contribution or fee is paid at the beginning of each semester, within the registration renewal period in the academic calendar. Students who do not pay on time, and whose excuse is not accepted by the unit's board, cannot register for that semester and cannot use their student rights.

## How to pay
Payments are made to Ziraat Bankası, the university's partner bank, using one of the options in the university's Collection Guide: https://www.bilecik.edu.tr/dosya/16518_a503_tahsilat%20rehberi%202023.pdf

## Refund after withdrawing
Students who registered and then withdrew at their own request do not get their contribution or fee back.

## Refund of an overpayment
If the student applies with a refund petition to the official at Student Affairs' Fees Office (Harçlar Şube Müdürlüğü), the extra amount paid is refunded to the bank account (IBAN) in the student's name given in the petition. This applies to extra payments of summer school fees, tuition contributions and tuition fees.

## Being in the top 10%
The list of second-education students in the top 10% is published every semester in the announcements on the Student Affairs website (https://bilecik.edu.tr/ogrenciisleri).

## Discount for students with disabilities
- Source: BŞEÜ 2026-2027 Contribution and Tuition Fee Table, Note 5
- Link: https://bilecik.edu.tr/ogrenciisleri/Icerik/Katkı_Payı_ve_Öğrenim_Ücretleri_8b80d
Students who submit a disability report to Student Affairs at registration or registration renewal get a discount on their tuition fee equal to their disability rate, with no limit on program duration or number of programs. First-education students in this group pay no contribution at all, with no limit on program duration or number of programs. A student who could not submit the report at registration gets the discount refunded from that year's payments on the date the report is submitted.

## Contribution during academic leave (registration freeze)

## Payment receipt
- Source: BŞEÜ Collection Guide (payment steps with Ziraat Bankası)
- Link: https://www.bilecik.edu.tr/dosya/41727_5d7c_28270_b59f_tahsilat rehberi.pdf
Payment is made through Ziraat Mobil, Ziraat internet banking or a Ziraat Bankası ATM (with or without a card): Payments > Education, Exams and Universities > University Fee and Exam Payments > Bilecik Şeyh Edebali Üniversitesi, then enter the Turkish ID number. The receipt (dekont) is taken when the transaction is finished: in Ziraat Mobil, download the receipt to your phone; in internet banking, press PRINT (YAZDIR) to print the receipt; at the ATM, do not forget to take the printed receipt.
