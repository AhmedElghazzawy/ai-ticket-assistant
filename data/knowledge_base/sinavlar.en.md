# Exams: Make-up (Bütünleme), Grade Objections, Excused Exams and the Single-Course Exam

- Source: BŞEÜ Ön Lisans ve Lisans Eğitim-Öğretim Yönetmeliği (Resmî Gazete no. 30824, 07.07.2019), Articles 19, 20, 21, 32
- Link: https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2019/07/20190707-1.htm
- Responsible office: the student's department / faculty; for the single-course exam also Student Affairs (Öğrenci İşleri Daire Başkanlığı)
- Note: This is an English summary of the Turkish regulation; the Turkish text is binding. Exam dates are announced in the academic calendar.

## Make-up exam / bütünleme (Article 19)
Students who had the right to take the final exam and failed it with an FF or YZ grade, and students who met the conditions but could not take the final exam, get a make-up exam (bütünleme) for each failed course without any application. Students cannot take the make-up exam to raise the grade of a course they already passed. No excuses are accepted for make-up exams. Students who did not earn the right to take the final exam cannot take the make-up exam.

## Exam schedule and taking exams (Article 19)
Exam schedules are announced in the student information system at least two weeks before the exams start. Students must come to the exam on the announced day, time and place, and bring their ID and any other required documents.

## Objecting to exam results (Article 20)
An objection must be made in writing to the teaching unit's management within five working days after the results are announced. Exam results are only changed to correct material errors made when adding up the grades. The unit's board decides on the objection.

## Excused (mazeret) exams (Article 21)
The excuse must be submitted in writing to the relevant unit within five working days from the start of the excuse; for medical reports, this period starts on the date the sick-leave report was issued. If the unit's board accepts the excuse, make-up exams for missed midterms are held in the thirteenth week of the semester. No excused exam is given for exams other than midterms. This means there is no excused exam for a missed final; a student who misses the final uses the make-up exam (bütünleme, Article 19).

## Single-course exam / tek ders sınavı (Article 32)
Students who met the attendance requirement in all courses and cannot graduate because they failed only one course (FF or YZ), or because their grade point average is too low, get one extra exam in one course on dates set in the academic calendar. Students who could not raise their GPA to 2.00 with this exam's grade do not get this right. Students must apply in writing to their unit at least five working days before the single-course exam date in the academic calendar. This right is given only once; a student who applies but does not take the exam cannot use it again.
