# Grades and GPA: Letter Grades, Grade Points and How the GPA (Genel Not Ortalaması) Is Calculated

- Source: BŞEÜ Ön Lisans ve Lisans Eğitim-Öğretim Yönetmeliği (Resmî Gazete no. 30824, 07.07.2019), Articles 22, 23, 24, 25
- Link: https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2019/07/20190707-1.htm
- Responsible office: the course's instructor and the student's department for grades; the Faculty/Vocational School student affairs office for the transcript
- Note: This is an English summary of the Turkish regulation; the Turkish text is binding. Grades and averages are shown in the student information system (OBS).

## Letter grades and grade points (Article 22)
A student's grade in a course is set by relative grading (bağıl değerlendirme): the instructor's numeric grade is converted into a letter grade, taking into account the class's overall success, the grade distribution and the class average. Letter grades and their grade points: AA 4.0; AB 3.7; BA 3.3; BB 3.0; BC 2.7; CB 2.3; CC 2.0; DC 1.5; DD 1.0; FF 0.0. For students who take the make-up exam (bütünleme), the letter grade is given according to the grade ranges formed after the course's letter grades were announced.

## Other grades: DZ, İZ, YT, YZ, GM, SD (Article 22)
DZ is given to a student who did not meet the attendance requirement; it counts like FF in the GPA, and the student cannot take that course's final or make-up exam and must repeat the course. İZ is given to a student who could not attend because of academic leave, and it does not count in the GPA. YT means passed and YZ means failed, in courses that do not count in the GPA. GM is given for an exam the student did not take; if the student does not take the excused (make-up) exam for a missed midterm, GM counts as zero. SD is given for courses removed by a curriculum transfer (intibak) that the student is no longer responsible for, and it does not count in the average.

## Letter grades on the 100-point scale (Article 23)
Letter grades are converted to the absolute system using the 4-point to 100-point equivalents set by the Council of Higher Education (YÖK): AA 93.01-100.00; AB 83.67-93.00; BA 76.67-83.66; BB 69.67-76.66; BC 60.34-69.66; CB 56.61-60.33; CC 53.33-56.60; DC 49.00-53.32; DD 45.00-48.99; FF 44.99 and below. This table gives the 100-point equivalent of a letter grade (for example, to convert a transcript to the 100-point scale); the letter grade in a course itself is given by relative grading.

## How the grade point average is calculated (Article 24)
The average is found by multiplying the grade points of each course that counts in the average by that course's credits, adding these products, and dividing the total by the sum of the courses' credits. Done for the courses of one semester, this gives the semester average (yarıyıl not ortalaması); done for all courses taken since entering the university, it gives the cumulative GPA (genel not ortalaması). Example: a student with BA (3.3) in a 5-credit course and CC (2.0) in a 3-credit course has an average of (5 × 3.3 + 3 × 2.0) / (5 + 3) = 22.5 / 8 = 2.81. When another course is repeated in place of an elective, only the grade of the course taken last counts in the average. Averages are shown with two decimal places. For students who change programs, the average includes all courses of the new program and only those earlier courses that the unit's board found suitable for the new department.

## The GPA needed to be successful (Article 25)
Associate and bachelor students must have a cumulative GPA of at least 2.00 to be successful; students below it are considered unsuccessful. Students repeat the courses in which they got FF, YZ or DZ (except courses whose prerequisite they did not meet) in the next academic year. For a student who was on academic leave or did not register for one semester, success is judged by the cumulative GPA at the end of the semester they studied, or of summer school if it is offered.
