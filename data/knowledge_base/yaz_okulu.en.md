# Summer School

- Source: BŞEÜ Yaz Okulu Yönetmeliği (Resmî Gazete 04.02.2009, no. 27131; last amended Resmî Gazete 12.07.2018, no. 30476), Articles 5, 6, 9, 10, 11, 14
- Link: https://bilecik.edu.tr/dosya/24873_e315_BİLECİK ŞEYH EDEBALİ ÜNİVERSİTESİ YAZ OKULU YÖNETMELİĞİ.pdf
- Responsible office: the student's department head; fees are set by the university's boards
- Note: This is an English summary of the Turkish regulation; the Turkish text is binding. Summer school dates are announced in the academic calendar.

## Purpose and opening of summer school (Articles 5, 6)
In summer school, students can retake failed courses, take courses to raise their GPA, and take courses they could not register for in the fall or spring semester; successful students can take upper-year courses. Summer school opens by decision of the university's boards. Summer school does not count toward the study period.

## Minimum number of students for a course (Article 9)
A summer school course opens only if at least twenty-five students (associate and bachelor programs) or five students (graduate programs) register. In necessary cases, the university's boards can change these numbers.

## Registration and course limit (Article 10)
Registering for summer school is voluntary. Students can register for at most four courses in total. Students with a GPA of 3.00 or higher can choose upper-year courses. Attendance is compulsory: 70% of theory classes and 80% of practical and laboratory classes. In the week before summer school starts, a student can add courses that are confirmed to open, in place of courses removed from their registration, within the four-course limit; but they cannot drop a course that is confirmed to open.

## Taking summer courses at another university (Article 10)
A compulsory course that is not opened or cannot be registered for in this university's summer school can be taken at another university's summer school, if the student has taken it at least once before and met the attendance requirement, and the department head approves its credits, hours and content. Students who can graduate at the end of summer school, or who have a GPA of 3.00 or higher, can also take compulsory courses they never took before at another university's summer school. How these grades are counted is set by the Course Exemption and Placement Directive.

## Grades, extra time and excused exams (Articles 11, 14)
Grades from summer school are included in the weighted GPA. A student with extra study time can take their courses in summer school to meet graduation requirements. In summer school, students who miss midterm and/or final exams for a reason accepted by the boards get an excused (mazeret) exam; for the same reason, at the student's request and by board decision, their summer school registration can be cancelled.
