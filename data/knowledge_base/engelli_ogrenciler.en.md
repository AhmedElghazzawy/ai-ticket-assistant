# Students with Disabilities: the Disabled Students Unit and Exam Arrangements

- Source: BŞEÜ Directive of the Disabled Students Unit Coordination Office (Engelli Öğrenciler Birim Koordinatörlüğü Yönergesi), Articles 5, 8, 11
- Link: https://www.bilecik.edu.tr/dosya/13141_f000_Bilecik Şeyh Edebali Üniversitesi Engelli Öğrenciler Birim Koordinatörlüğü Yönergesi.pdf
- Responsible office: Disabled Students Unit Coordination Office; the disabled-students unit representative at the student's faculty or school
- Note: This is an English summary of the Turkish directive; the Turkish text is binding. The directive lists the kinds of exam arrangements, but does not set the amount of extra time.

## What the Disabled Students Unit is (Article 5)
The Disabled Students Unit Coordination Office is the unit set up within the university to make arrangements that give students with disabilities comfortable education conditions, to identify and meet their needs, and to take the necessary measures. The unit reports directly to the Rector's Office (Article 6).

## Extra time and other exam arrangements (Article 8)
One of the unit's duties is: so that all students are measured and evaluated fairly and correctly, to ensure equal opportunity and make education meaningful for students with disabilities too, to provide the student with disabilities with arrangements for exams regarding time, place, materials and an accompanying reader, and to take the necessary measures according to the nature of the disability. So a student with a disability can be given extra exam time, a separate room, suitable materials or a reader; which arrangement is made depends on the kind of disability.

## Applying: the unit representative (Article 11)
At every faculty, institute, school and vocational school, a lecturer appointed by the dean or director is the disabled-students unit representative, and at the start of each academic year identifies the students with disabilities in their academic unit. The representative identifies the needs and problems that students with disabilities face in the academic unit and reports them to the coordination office. The unit also identifies students with disabilities at registration (Article 8). For the contribution and tuition fee discount for students with disabilities, see the document 'Tuition Contribution (Katkı Payı), Tuition Fees and Refunds'.
