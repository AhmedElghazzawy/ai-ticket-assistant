# Internships (Faculty of Engineering)

- Source: BŞEÜ Mühendislik Fakültesi Öğrenci Staj Yönergesi (Senate decision 03.05.2012, revised 25.12.2019, 230/2), Articles 8-18
- Link: https://www.bilecik.edu.tr/dosya/13520_82b1_Staj yönergesi.pdf
- Responsible office: the Department Internship Commission and the Department Head
- Note: This applies only to the Faculty of Engineering; other faculties have their own internship directives. Details are in each department's internship guide. This directive does not cover SGK insurance registration; ask the department about it. This is an English summary; the Turkish text is binding.

## Length and timing (Article 13)
Students can start an internship only after at least four semesters in their department. The internship needed for graduation is 40 working days in total, in two parts of 20 working days each. The Department Internship Commission decides when internships can be done; mainly when there are no classes. Students in their graduating term can start in any month with the commission's approval. Students taking summer school cannot do their internship during summer school; any overlap must not exceed 3 working days. Official holidays do not count as internship days.

## Finding a place and applying (Articles 9, 10, 11)
Departments are not obliged to find internship places for students. Places offered to the department are announced and distributed by the Department Internship Commission; if there are not enough, students can use a company they find themselves if the commission approves it. No student can do an internship at a company they found themselves without the commission's approval. The student has the acceptance letter from the company approved by the commission, and then receives the official internship letter and the forms in the guide. Internships abroad are possible through ERASMUS or with the commission's written approval.

## Documents and report deadline (Articles 8, 14)
The student prepares the application petition, the evaluation form (filled in by the workplace supervisor) and the internship report. The evaluation form is sent to the department at the end of the internship in a sealed, stamped envelope. The report must be approved with the supervisor's stamp and signature; reports without signature or stamp, or with erasures, are not accepted. Students who submit their report after the announced date do not get their internship accepted. The report must be submitted by the date the commission sets, within the academic semester after the internship.

## Attendance, illness and accidents (Articles 12, 15, 16)
Attending the internship is compulsory; changing the internship place without the commission's approval makes the internship invalid. An intern absent three days in a row without permission or excuse is removed from the internship. If a student is ill for more than three days or has an accident, the internship is stopped; the missed days are added to the internship, but this can never be more than half of the total length.

## Evaluation and internship exemption (Articles 17, 18)
Internships are evaluated within one month at the latest, by reviewing the report or, if needed, by an oral exam. The result is SUCCESSFUL, PARTLY SUCCESSFUL or UNSUCCESSFUL; the student repeats the failed part. For horizontal or vertical transfer students, up to 20 working days of a documented internship from their previous university can be accepted by commission decision.
