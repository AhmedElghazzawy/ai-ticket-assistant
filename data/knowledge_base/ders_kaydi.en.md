# Registration Renewal, Course Registration and Add/Drop

- Source: BŞEÜ Ön Lisans ve Lisans Eğitim-Öğretim Yönetmeliği (Resmî Gazete no. 30824, 07.07.2019), Articles 8, 15, 16
- Link: https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2019/07/20190707-1.htm
- Responsible office: Student Affairs (Öğrenci İşleri Daire Başkanlığı)
- Note: This is an English summary of the Turkish regulation; the Turkish text is binding. Dates are announced each year in the academic calendar.

## Registration renewal (Article 8)
Students must renew their registration at the beginning of the fall and spring semesters, within the period given in the academic calendar. Students with a valid excuse can complete it by the end of the first week of the semester. Students are responsible for their own registration renewal.

## Course registration and credit load (Article 15)
In the first two semesters of their program, students must take all courses offered, provided they meet any prerequisite. The credit load a student may take can depend on their grade point average. Vertical and horizontal transfer students may register for up to 50% more than the normal credit load in their first semester.

## Adding, dropping and changing courses (Article 16)
With their advisor's approval, students can change, add and drop courses within the first week after classes start. These changes must stay within the set credit limits.
