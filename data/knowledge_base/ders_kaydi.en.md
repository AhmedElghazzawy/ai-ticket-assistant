# Registration Renewal, Course Registration and Add/Drop

- Source: BŞEÜ Ön Lisans ve Lisans Eğitim-Öğretim Yönetmeliği (Resmî Gazete no. 30824, 07.07.2019), Articles 8, 15, 16
- Link: https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2019/07/20190707-1.htm
- Responsible office: Student Affairs (Öğrenci İşleri Daire Başkanlığı); the student's advisor for course selection
- Note: This is an English summary of the Turkish regulation; the Turkish text is binding. Dates are announced each year in the academic calendar.

## Registration renewal (Article 8)
Students must renew their registration at the beginning of the fall and spring semesters, within the period given in the academic calendar. Students who could not renew in time, and whose excuse is accepted by the unit's board under the Directive on Justified and Valid Reasons (Haklı ve Geçerli Nedenler Yönergesi), can renew by the end of the first week of the semester. Students are responsible for all registration renewal steps. A semester in which a student did not renew still counts toward their study period.

## Course registration and credit load (Article 15)
In the first two semesters of their program, students must take all courses offered, provided they meet any prerequisite. Bachelor students with a GPA below 2.00 (1.75 for associate degrees) can register for the normal credit load. Bachelor students with a GPA of 2.00 or higher (1.75 for associate degrees) can register for up to 50% more than the normal credit load. Bachelor students with a GPA below 3.00 cannot register for upper-year courses; after the first two semesters, bachelor students with no failed or untaken courses and a GPA of 3.00 or higher can take upper-year courses with their advisor's approval. Students should choose lower-semester courses first.

## Adding, dropping and changing courses (Article 16)
With their advisor's approval, students can change, add and drop courses within the first week after classes start, within the credit limits of Article 15. A student cannot attend or take the exam of a course they did not properly register for in time; if they take the exam anyway, the grade is cancelled.
