# Attendance, Maximum Study Period and Graduation

- Source: BŞEÜ Ön Lisans ve Lisans Eğitim-Öğretim Yönetmeliği (Resmî Gazete no. 30824, 07.07.2019), Articles 12, 17, 31, 33, 34
- Link: https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2019/07/20190707-1.htm
- Responsible office: Student Affairs (Öğrenci İşleri Daire Başkanlığı); the course instructor for attendance
- Note: This is an English summary of the Turkish regulation; the Turkish text is binding.

## Attendance requirement (Article 17)
Students must attend 70% of theory classes and 80% of practical, laboratory, workshop and studio classes. A student who does not meet the attendance requirement gets the grade DZ for that course. Medical reports taken during the semester do not count as attendance. Before the final exams, the instructor announces in the student information system which students cannot take the exam because of absences. Students assigned to official duties by the rectorate, dean's office or directorate count as present during their duty.

## Normal and maximum study period (Articles 12 and 31)
The normal length of a bachelor's program is four academic years (eight semesters), and of an associate program two academic years (four semesters). Excluding the preparatory year, and regardless of whether the student registered each semester, two-year associate programs must be completed within at most four years, four-year bachelor programs within seven years, five-year programs within eight years and six-year programs within nine years.

## When the maximum period ends (Articles 31 and 33)
A student is not dismissed immediately when the maximum period ends. Students who have completed the maximum period get two extra exams for all courses graded FF or YZ, provided they had earned the right to take the final exam and they apply; no excused exam is given for these. Students who, after the extra exams, have five or fewer failed or never-taken courses get three extra semesters. Students who have at most five failed or never-taken courses without taking the extra exams get four extra semesters. A student with only one course left to graduate gets unlimited rights to take that course's final exams. Among students who have completed the maximum period, those who still have more than five courses left to graduate after using the extra exams, or more than five never-taken or absence-failed courses, are dismissed (Article 33/6). Students are not dismissed within the maximum period for not paying fees or not renewing registration (Article 33).

## Graduation requirements (Article 34)
To finish the program and receive a diploma, a student needs a GPA of at least 2.00, at least a DD or YT grade in all courses, and must pass the required internships. Associate students must also pass at least 120 ECTS and bachelor students at least 240 ECTS. A student who qualifies to graduate can repeat courses to raise their GPA in summer school or in the next two semesters; they must apply with a petition to the department head by the end of the make-up exams of the semester in which they qualified. Graduation becomes final with a decision of the unit's board.
