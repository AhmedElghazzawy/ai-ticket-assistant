# Academic Leave (Freezing Registration) and Withdrawing from the University

- Source: BŞEÜ Ön Lisans ve Lisans Eğitim-Öğretim Yönetmeliği (Resmî Gazete no. 30824, 07.07.2019), Articles 36 and 33
- Link: https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2019/07/20190707-1.htm
- Responsible office: the student's dean's office / school directorate; the decision is reported to Student Affairs (Öğrenci İşleri Daire Başkanlığı)
- Note: This is an English summary of the Turkish regulation; the Turkish text is binding.

## How to apply for academic leave (Article 36)
The student submits a petition giving the reason, with supporting documents, to their dean's office or school directorate. The unit's board evaluates the request under the Directive on Justified and Valid Reasons (Haklı ve Geçerli Nedenler Yönergesi), and the decision is sent in writing to the student and to Student Affairs.

## Application period (Article 36)
Academic leave should be requested within the first four weeks of the semester. Except for sudden illness and unexpected situations, applications after this period are not processed. For reasons that could not be foreseen, such as natural disasters, detention, conviction or the lifting of a military service deferral, leave can also be given during the semester; the student then counts as on leave from the start of the semester.

## Academic leave for health reasons (Article 36)
For health-related leave, the medical report must cover at least twenty-two continuous or consecutive teaching days. A student on health leave for more than one semester can re-register by board decision if a report from a secondary or tertiary health institution shows they can continue their studies.

## Length and effects of academic leave (Article 36)
Students can get academic leave for at most two semesters at a time, and up to four semesters in total. Students who document a permanent health problem with a report from a secondary or tertiary health institution are exempt from this limit. Students on academic leave cannot attend classes or take exams, and the leave period does not count toward their study period. Courses a student had registered for before taking leave get the grade İZ (Article 17).

## Withdrawing at your own request (Article 33)
Students who want to leave apply in writing to their unit. Students who leave the university at their own request lose all student rights. If they want to return, they go through the same process as new applicants. On request, a withdrawn student receives a document showing their academic record and the diploma and other documents they submitted at registration.
