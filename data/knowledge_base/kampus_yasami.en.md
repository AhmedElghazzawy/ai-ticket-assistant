## Starting a student club (Articles 7, 8)
A club needs at least fifteen founding students. The club advisor sends the application with the required documents to the SKS office by the last day of October or February each year. Applications made outside October/February, or with missing documents, are invalid. Required documents: Club Founding Petition Form (EK-1), Club Statute (EK-2), current student certificates of the founding students, and documents showing they have no disciplinary penalty. Two clubs cannot have the same name.

## Joining a club (Article 12)
Membership starts when the student fills in the membership form (EK-5) and is completed after the Club Board's decision and the club advisor's approval. Students with a disciplinary penalty cannot join any club. Students can be members of more than one club, but can serve on the board or audit committee of only one club.

## Leaving a club (Article 13)
A student can leave a club by giving a petition to the Club Board.

## Library and the 24/7 study hall (Orientation 2025-2026)
- Source: BŞEÜ 2025-2026 Orientation Presentation
- Link: https://bilecik.edu.tr/dosya/33190_3511_Oryantasyon - Çocuk Gelişimi Bölümü 2025-2026.pdf

## Cafeteria and topping up the smart card (Orientation 2025-2026)
- Source: BŞEÜ 2025-2026 Orientation Presentation
- Link: https://bilecik.edu.tr/dosya/33190_3511_Oryantasyon - Çocuk Gelişimi Bölümü 2025-2026.pdf
Students who want to eat in the university cafeterias must load money onto their smart student card (Akıllı Kart). Money is loaded at unka.bilecik.edu.tr by logging in with the Turkish ID number and paying by credit card. You can also load money at the kiosk machines at cafeteria or building entrances.

## Dormitories (KYK)
- Source: Non-official source: kariyer.net Bilecik KYK dormitory guide
- Link: https://www.kariyer.net/kyk-yurt-rehberi/bilecik
Student dormitories in Bilecik belong to the state (Kredi ve Yurtlar Kurumu, KYK), not to the university; for example the Halime Hatun, Pazaryeri and Bilecik KYK dormitories for women and the Ertuğrulgazi and Şeyh Edebali KYK dormitories for men. For dormitory applications, room changes, dormitory fees and repairs, contact KYK; the university does not handle these. (This section is based on a non-official source.)

## Borrowing books from the library
- Source: BŞEÜ Library and Documentation Department, Library Rules and FAQ
- Link: https://bilecik.edu.tr/kutuphane/Icerik/kutuphane-kullanim-kurallari-ve-sss
Students become library members with their BŞEÜ ID card and/or Turkish ID card and borrow items for themselves; the BŞEÜ ID card must be used for borrowing. Associate and bachelor students can borrow 5 books for 20 days and renew them twice for 20 days (master's and doctoral students: 10 books, 30 days). Renewal becomes possible in the last 5 days of the loan. Renew through the library automation system or at the library; renewal by e-mail is not possible. Late fines start on the 3rd day of delay: 4 TL on the first day, then 4 TL more each day; when the debt reaches 100 TL new loans are blocked, and the fine stops growing at 200 TL per item. A locker is given to a student for 7 days.

## Booking a group study room
- Source: BŞEÜ Library and Documentation Department, Group Study Room and Booking Rules
- Link: https://bilecik.edu.tr/kutuphane/Icerik/Grup_Çalışma_Odası_Ve_Rezervasyon_Kuralları_ba93f
Book at https://katalog.bilecik.edu.tr/ under My Account (Hesabım) > Study Space Booking (Çalışma Alanı Rezervasyon) with the "Rezervasyon Yap" button; collect the room key at the Information Desk (Danışma) with your student ID card. Group study rooms are for groups of at least 3 people (at least 5 during exam weeks). Bookings are made in 2-hour slots, and one user can book at most 2 hours a day. Rooms can be booked from 7 days before until 15 minutes before the start. If the key is not collected within 15 minutes of the start, the booking is cancelled; a user who books and does not come, or returns the key late, loses booking and borrowing rights for 7 days. Users are responsible for their own belongings; the library is not responsible for lost or stolen items.
