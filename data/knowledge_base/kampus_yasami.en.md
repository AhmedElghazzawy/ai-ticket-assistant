# Campus Life: Student Clubs and Dormitories

- Source: BŞEÜ Öğrenci Kulüpleri Yönergesi (Senate decision 30.06.2021, 295/8), Articles 4, 7, 8, 12, 13; for dormitories, a non-official source: kariyer.net Bilecik KYK dormitory guide

- Responsible office: Health, Culture and Sports Office (Sağlık, Kültür ve Spor Daire Başkanlığı, SKS)
- Note: This is an English summary of the Turkish directive; the Turkish text is binding. This document has no information on the cafeteria, psychological counseling or sports facilities; for those, contact the SKS office.

## Starting a student club (Articles 7, 8)
A club needs at least fifteen founding students. The club advisor sends the application with the required documents to the SKS office by the last day of October or February each year. Applications made outside October/February, or with missing documents, are invalid. Required documents: Club Founding Petition Form (EK-1), Club Statute (EK-2), current student certificates of the founding students, and documents showing they have no disciplinary penalty. Two clubs cannot have the same name.

## Joining a club (Article 12)
Membership starts when the student fills in the membership form (EK-5) and is completed after the Club Board's decision and the club advisor's approval. Students with a disciplinary penalty cannot join any club. Students can be members of more than one club, but can serve on the board or audit committee of only one club.

## Leaving a club (Article 13)
A student can leave a club by giving a petition to the Club Board.

## Dormitories (KYK)
Student dormitories in Bilecik belong to the state (Kredi ve Yurtlar Kurumu, KYK), not to the university; for example the Halime Hatun, Pazaryeri and Bilecik KYK dormitories for women and the Ertuğrulgazi and Şeyh Edebali KYK dormitories for men. For dormitory applications, room changes, dormitory fees and repairs, contact KYK; the university does not handle these. (This section is based on a non-official source.)
