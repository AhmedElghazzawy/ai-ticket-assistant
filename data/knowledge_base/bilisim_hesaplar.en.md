# IT Accounts: SOFRA, Student E-mail, OBS, UZEM and eduroam

- Source: BŞEÜ 2025-2026 Orientation Presentation (Child Development Department; these sections apply to all university systems)
- Link: https://bilecik.edu.tr/dosya/33190_3511_Oryantasyon - Çocuk Gelişimi Bölümü 2025-2026.pdf
- Responsible office: IT Department (Bilgi İşlem Daire Başkanlığı); for ÖBS login problems, Student Affairs (Öğrenci İşleri Daire Başkanlığı)
- Note: This is an English summary of the Turkish presentation; the Turkish text is binding.

## What SOFRA is and how to log in
The student e-mail account is created through the SOFRA system (https://sofra.bilecik.edu.tr/). To access SOFRA, use the same login details as for ÖBS (https://obs.bilecik.edu.tr/). If you have problems logging in to the student information system, ask Student Affairs. After logging in to SOFRA, the e-mail and phone details under Kimlik Bilgilerim > Hesabım and Güvenlik in the left menu must be up to date.

## Creating the student e-mail account
E-mail accounts are created with the "Hesabımı Oluştur" (Create My Account) button on the Öğrenci E-posta (Student E-mail) tab in SOFRA. You then log in to the mail system at https://ogrenci.bilecik.edu.tr/ with your new @ogrenci.bilecik.edu.tr address and the password you use in the student information system (ÖBS).

## Logging in to the Student Information System (OBS)
You can access the student information system at https://obs.bilecik.edu.tr/login.aspx and log in with the password you created through SOFRA, or through e-Devlet.

## Distance education system (UZEM)
Atatürk Principles and History of Revolutions, Basic Information Technology, English and Turkish Language courses are run on UZEM. You log in at https://ders.bilecik.edu.tr/ with the username and password provided by the university. For your password to become active, you must log in once to the Student Information System at http://obs.bilecik.edu.tr/.

## Wireless internet (eduroam)
To use the wireless internet, after your e-mail account is opened, log in at portal.bilecik.edu.tr with your username and password and click the "Yeni Kullanıcı Bilgilerimi Oluştur" (Create My New User Details) button. You can connect directly to the eduroam wireless network with your Student Information System (OBS) username and password.
