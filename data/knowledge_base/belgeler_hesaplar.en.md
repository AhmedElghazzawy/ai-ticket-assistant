# Student Number, OBS Password, Documents and the Student ID Card

- Source: BŞEÜ Student Affairs (Öğrenci İşleri Daire Başkanlığı), Frequently Asked Questions: Diğer İşlemler, Akıllı Kart İşlemleri, Mezuniyet İşlemleri, Kayıt Silme İşlemleri

## Student number
The university uses the Turkish ID number (T.C. kimlik numarası) as the student number. International students use their YÖK number, or their YU number if they have one.

## OBS password
New students automatically receive their password at the mobile phone number and e-mail address they gave at registration. For any problem, ask the student affairs office of your faculty or vocational school.

## Student certificate and transcript
The student applies with a petition to the student affairs office of their academic unit. The officer prints the document from OBS with the unit head's signature and stamp. The student certificate and transcript can also be obtained through e-Devlet. A document showing there is no obstacle to a horizontal transfer is obtained the same way.

## Graduation certificate
If the diploma is delayed for unforeseen reasons, a "Graduation Certificate" (Mezun Belgesi) can be obtained through e-Devlet.

## After withdrawing: e-Devlet
After the withdrawal is done in the Student Information System, the data in YÖKSİS is updated one day later.

## Lost or damaged student ID card (Akıllı Kart)
Pay the ID card fee to the university's Ziraat Bankası account (IBAN: TR39 0001 0001 1848 3433 4750 01) with the description "Kart Basım Bedeli". Students who come to Student Affairs with the receipt get a newly printed student ID card. Note: account details can change; check the Student Affairs website before paying.

## Graduation date
- Link: https://bilecik.edu.tr/ogrenciisleri/Icerik/Mezuniyet_tarihi_nasil_belirlenir_
For associate and bachelor programs, the graduation date written on the diploma is the date of the meeting at which the board of the student's academic unit decided on the graduation.

## Collecting the diploma and diploma supplement
- Link: https://bilecik.edu.tr/ogrenciisleri/Icerik/Diploma_ve_diploma_eki_teslimi_nasil_yapilir_
The diploma and diploma supplement are handed over only to the graduates themselves, or to their representatives who show a notarized power of attorney. If the graduate is abroad, the power of attorney must be issued by a consulate of the Republic of Türkiye. Once the clearance (ilişik kesme) process is completed, the diploma, diploma supplement, transcript and high-school diploma are handed to the graduate against signature, after the graduate fills in the diploma register. The diploma is not sent by post, and requests by phone or fax are not accepted. If the diploma is delayed for unforeseen reasons, a Graduation Certificate (Mezun Belgesi) can be obtained from e-Devlet (see Graduation certificate).

## Diploma supplement
- Link: https://bilecik.edu.tr/ogrenciisleri/Icerik/Diploma_eki_nedir_ve_nasil_alabilirim_
The Diploma Supplement, developed by the Council of Europe, the European Commission and UNESCO/CEPES, is a document that higher-education institutions give in addition to the diploma so that the degree can be easily understood. It must be given free of charge together with the diploma, without the student asking for it, and like the diploma it is issued only once, as a single copy. It also lists the courses the student took and their grades, and helps the diploma to be understood abroad for study or work.
