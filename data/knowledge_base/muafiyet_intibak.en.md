# Course Exemption, Placement (İntibak) and Opening Courses

- Source: BŞEÜ Ders Açma, Muafiyet ve İntibak Esasları Yönergesi (Senate decision 12.12.2013, last amended 12.10.2022), Articles 5 and 6
- Link: https://www.bilecik.edu.tr/dosya/13141_43dd_Bilecik Şeyh Edebali Üniversitesi Ders Açma, Muafiyet ve İntibak Esasları Yönergesi.pdf
- Responsible office: the student's faculty / school (the Placement Commission and the unit's board)
- Note: This is an English summary of the Turkish directive; the Turkish text is binding.

## How an exemption is decided (Article 6)
The Placement Commission (İntibak Komisyonu) reviews the request and prepares a placement program. The unit's board decides, within one week at the latest, on all courses the student passed earlier with a letter grade of CC or higher and asked to be exempted from. Until the board decides, students keep attending the courses they asked to be exempted from.

## Exemption conditions (Article 6)
The course to be exempted, whether compulsory or elective, must have an equal or higher ECTS value or an equal or higher number of class hours; the match of course content and learning outcomes is also checked. Several passed courses together can count for one course under the same condition. Atatürk Principles and History of Revolutions, Turkish Language and English passed in open or distance education programs are exempted.

## Year placement and objections (Article 6)
If the ECTS total of the exempted courses is 60% or more of the year's ECTS load, the student is placed in the next year. Horizontal transfer students are placed in the year they were admitted to, and vertical transfer students in the third year. Objections to exemption and placement results must be made to the unit within 5 working days after the result is announced to the student.

## Courses taken at another university, in exchange programs or in summer school (Article 6)
Grades of courses taken at another higher-education institution as a special student, in an exchange program (e.g. Erasmus) or in summer school are converted to the university's grade equivalents. If one course here is matched with several courses there, their average is used; if the student failed any of them, they also fail the course here.

## When an elective is not opened (Article 5)
Electives open only if at least 10 students choose them. If fewer students choose an elective, it is closed, and the registration advisors move those students to another opened elective according to the students' wishes. Compulsory courses open regardless of the number of students.
