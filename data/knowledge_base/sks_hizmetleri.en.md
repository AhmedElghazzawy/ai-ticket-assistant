## Cafeteria opening hours
- Source: BŞEÜ SKS Department, Nutrition Services: Frequently Asked Questions and Meal Times
- Link: https://bilecik.edu.tr/sks/Icerik/Sıkça_Sorulan_Sorular_5095a
In the central campus cafeterias, during the teaching period, lunch and dinner are served from the same menu, 11:00-14:00 and 16:00-18:00. Outside the teaching period and during summer school, the cafeterias serve 11:00-14:00. On district campuses, schools without evening education serve 11:00-14:00, and schools with evening education serve 11:00-14:00 and 16:00-18:00. During Ramadan, lunch is 11:00-14:00, dinner 16:00-17:00 and at iftar time. The monthly menu is published on the SKS page (Yemek Menüsü).

## Meal price and topping up the card
- Source: BŞEÜ SKS Department, Meal Prices and Nutrition Services FAQ
- Link: https://bilecik.edu.tr/sks/Icerik/Yemek_Ücretleri_67696
From 08.07.2026, the student meal contribution is 50.00 TL for associate and bachelor students and 60.00 TL for master's and doctoral students. You can eat more than once a day with your student ID card, but only the first meal is at the discounted price; a second or further meal is charged at the meal's cost. Balance is topped up online (unka.bilecik.edu.tr) with your Turkish ID number.

## Diet, allergy and gluten-free meals
- Source: BŞEÜ SKS Department, Nutrition Services: Frequently Asked Questions
- Link: https://bilecik.edu.tr/sks/Icerik/Sıkça_Sorulan_Sorular_5095a
If a student or staff member who must follow a special diet informs the SKS Department of their situation in writing, the department can have the contractor prepare diet menus under suitable conditions and serve them. This also applies to students with allergies or gluten intolerance (celiac disease): inform the SKS Department in writing. Menus are prepared monthly by the university's dietitian and food engineer.

## Meal scholarship
- Source: BŞEÜ SKS Department, Meal Scholarship
- Link: https://bilecik.edu.tr/sks/Icerik/Yemek_Bursu_810a0
Under the university's Meal Scholarship Directive, students in need can eat one meal a day for free in the university cafeterias for one academic year. The conditions are in the Meal Scholarship Directive; the application period is announced in SKS announcements.

## Part-time work scholarship
- Source: BŞEÜ SKS Department, Health and Social Services: Frequently Asked Questions
- Link: https://bilecik.edu.tr/sks/Icerik/Sıkça_Sorulan_Sorular_469bb
Part-time work means a student works in university units for a set wage during one academic year; places are limited. Working time cannot exceed 5 hours a day, 15 hours a week and 60 hours a month. Main conditions: active registration at the university, no disciplinary penalty, no income at minimum-wage level, not on a registration freeze, not a foreign national, and studying within the normal study period. Part-time students are recruited at the start of each academic year, in a set application period (October). The student applies in person, with the required documents, to the unit they want to work in during the application dates. Working part-time at the university does not cut any of your other scholarships. Under the Part-Time Student Work Directive, applications from students who have exceeded their normal study period are not considered.

## Sports facilities and prices
- Source: BŞEÜ SKS Department, Sports Services: Frequently Asked Questions, Facilities and 2026 Price List
- Link: https://bilecik.edu.tr/sks/Icerik/Sıkça_Sorulan_Sorular_cb57c
The central campus has an indoor sports hall, a football pitch with an athletics track, a squash hall, an outdoor tennis court, outdoor volleyball-basketball courts, an indoor artificial-turf pitch (halı saha), a fitness center (Healthy Living Center) and a climbing wall. All facilities except fitness and the artificial-turf pitch are free. For information on using the sports facilities, ask the information desk of the indoor sports hall. 2026 prices: fitness monthly membership 2,080.00 TL, artificial-turf pitch 1,200.00 TL per hour; free for students in their course program and for athletes in university teams. The Healthy Living Center is open 08:00-17:00. The artificial-turf pitch is open 24 hours by appointment via internal phone 1807; the fee is paid from the smart card. Trials for university teams are held at the start of each academic year; no athlete license is needed.

## Psychological counselling
- Source: BŞEÜ SKS Department, Psychological Counselling Services
- Link: https://bilecik.edu.tr/sks/Icerik/Psikolojik_Danismanlik_Hizmetleri
The SKS Department offers individual psychological counselling to students and staff; sessions are based on confidentiality, voluntariness and trust. Counselling sessions are by appointment. Appointments can be made through the unit's e-mail address, the unit phone (0228 214 26 05), or by visiting the Psychological Counselling Unit. The Psychological Counselling Unit serves the university's students and staff free of charge.

## Conference halls
- Source: BŞEÜ SKS Department, Culture Services: Conference Halls and Frequently Asked Questions
- Link: https://bilecik.edu.tr/sks/Icerik/Sıkça_Sorulan_Sorular_9c141
Conference halls are managed by the SKS Department's Culture Services Office; hall requests are made to this department. Halls: Faculty of Economics and Administrative Sciences Conference Hall (147 seats, ground floor), Faculty of Human and Social Sciences E Block Conference Hall (148 seats, 2nd floor), Faculty of Science F Block Conference Hall (148 seats, 2nd floor). All three have central air conditioning, a projector, internet and a sound system. The page does not say how many days before the event the request must be made.
